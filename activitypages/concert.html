Stout & Howard Concert & Fireworks

Close out Winterfest with a free family concert before and after the Light Parade, followed by fireworks over La Verkin.

  • Date — Saturday, December 5, 2026
  • Concert — Before and after the Light Parade (6:30 PM on the schedule)
  • Location — Outside stage, La Verkin ballfield
  • Cost — Free
  • Fireworks — Immediately after the concert, around 7:00 PM

Fireworks

  • Launched from the La Verkin Overlook right after the concert
  • Please bring your pets in for the evening
