Gingerbread House Contest

Everyone is welcome to enter, with houses made from any materials you like. Compete for the $200 grand prize!

Questions? Call (435) 669-3325
  • Entries due — Wednesday, December 2, 6:00–8:00 PM
  • Drop off at — 111 S Main St., La Verkin
  • Winners announced — Friday, December 4 at the Tree Lighting
  • Who can enter — Everyone

Prizes

  • $200 grand prize
  • Youth and family division winners get prizes too

What to know

  • Houses can be made from any materials you like
  • Bring your finished house to 111 S Main St. on Wednesday, December 2 between 6 and 8 PM
