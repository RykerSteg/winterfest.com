Tree Lighting & Sweater Fun Run

Kick off Winterfest Friday evening with the Family Christmas Sweater Fun Run, then join us for the grand opening “L” & Tree Lighting Ceremony.

  • Date — Friday, December 4, 2026
  • Tree Lighting — 5:00 PM at 111 S Main St. (outdoor)
  • Sweater judging — 4:00 PM at Wanlass Park
  • Fun Run — 4:30 PM, Wanlass Park to the Tree Lighting

At the Tree Lighting

  • Elementary choir performance
  • Meet the Grand Marshals
  • Gingerbread House Contest winners announced
  • Fun Run sweater winners announced
