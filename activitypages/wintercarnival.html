Activities

Winter Carnival

Free entrance! A full carnival of winter fun for the whole family, both days of Winterfest.

  • Dates — December 4 & 5, 2026
  • Hours — Friday from 5:30 PM · Saturday from 11:00 AM
  • Location — Behind the white building, 111 S Main St.
  • Cost — Free entrance

At the carnival

  • Photos with Santa
  • Storytime
  • Sock skating rink
  • “Snow” ball fight
  • Wagon ride and barrel train ride
  • Build a snowman and the snowball pit
  • Bounce house and slide

Wreaths Across America

  • Donations are accepted at the carnival to place a wreath on a veteran’s grave
