Winterfest 5K & 10K Race

Kick off Winterfest with a run through La Verkin on Saturday, December 5. Register by November 12 for the early price — no same-day registration.

Register for the race
  • Distances — 5K and 10K
  • Date — Saturday, December 5, 2026
  • Race start — 9:00 AM
  • Location — Wanlass Park, 320 N Main St., La Verkin
  • Sign up in person — La Verkin City Office, 435 N Main St.

Cost to participate

  • $25 Through November 12
  • $30 November 13 – December 4

Race morning

  • 7:30 AM — Packet pick-up at Wanlass Park
  • 8:15 AM — Treasure hunt from our sponsors (runners only)
  • 8:40 AM — Warm-up
  • 9:00 AM — Race begins

What to know

  • No same-day registration — sign up by December 4
  • Early packet pick-up: Friday, December 4, 5:00–8:00 PM at the La Verkin City Offices
