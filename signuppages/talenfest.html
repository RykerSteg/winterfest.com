Talentfest Charity Showcase

’Tis the season to give back! Share your talent at Winterfest for a chance to win $300 for the charity of your choice. Applications are due November 10 and spots are limited.

Sign up to perform
  • Applications due — November 10 (spots are limited)
  • Performances — December 4 & 5, 2026
  • Location — 111 S. Main St., La Verkin
  • Awards ceremony — December 5 at 1:15 PM (voting closes at 1:00 PM)
  • Who can enter — Open to all: musicians, singers, dancers, rappers, actors, comedians, magicians, and more

Prizes

  • $300 To the charity of the audience’s favorite act
  • $300 To the charity of the committee’s pick
  • $100 Drawing for every performer — must be present at the awards to win

How Talentfest works

  1. Sign up to share your talent and support a charity of your choice. Eligible charities include a 501(c)(3), a verified GoFundMe page, or a cause covered in a verified news article.
  2. You’ll be given a designated time to perform on stage during Winterfest.
  3. Share your cause and your performance time with friends and family.
  4. QR codes will be posted at Winterfest so the audience can vote for their favorite performer and charity.
  5. Everyone who performs is entered into a drawing for $100. You must be present at the awards ceremony to win.
  6. Two acts will each win $300 for their charity: one chosen by audience votes, one chosen by the Winterfest committee.
  7. Voting closes at 1:00 PM on December 5. Winners are announced at the awards ceremony at 1:15 PM.
  8. Applications are due November 10, and spots are limited.
